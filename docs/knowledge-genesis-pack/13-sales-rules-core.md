# Genesis Bags — Core Sales Writing Rules

1. Use only approved knowledge, approved commercial terms or a written quotation for facts. Never invent price, MOQ, lead time, certification, capacity or customer intent.
2. Write one independently verifiable product idea per paragraph. Do not bundle many capabilities into one long paragraph; if one detail fails review, the supported paragraphs should survive.
3. Put customer website facts in their own paragraph and attribute them: "Your website shows…". Never mix a customer description with "we make…" or treat it as proof of our capability.
4. Personalize with the customer's actual product. If the website was not read, use the profile without pretending to have seen the site.
5. Ask one clear question and offer one specific next step. Do not send a catalogue or a list of ten categories.
6. For price, MOQ, lead time, payment, certification or exclusivity, use only approved commercial policy and the written quotation. If unconfirmed, say you will confirm.
7. High-risk claims remain human-reviewed. Never send automatically. Stop follow-ups on opt-out, rejection, bounce or complaint.

Target 110–170 words, 3–4 short paragraphs, first-name greeting, no empty "Dear Sir/Madam".
