# Genesis Bags — Certifications, FSC/GRS/GOTS and EU PPWR

Source: https://www.genesisbags.com/about/, /products/eco-friendly-packaging/, /blog/eu-ppwr-packaging-guide/, captured 2026-10-26.

IMPORTANT: This document records claims published on the Genesis website. It is not a certificate, an audit report or a legal compliance opinion. Before relying on any certification in a customer commitment, obtain the current certificate, check its scope/products and validity dates, and confirm it covers the specific material and production site.

## Published certification status

- ISO 9001 quality management.
- FSC® Chain of Custody certification for paper and board; Genesis states it can supply FSC-certified paper/board with material traceability documentation.
- BSCI social-compliance audited.
- GRS (Global Recycled Standard) certification for recycled content of synthetic fabrics is referenced for recycled materials; confirm the exact scope for a specific fabric.
- GOTS-eligible organic cotton and GRS-certified recycled cotton variants are available on request; "GOTS-eligible" is not the same as a GOTS-certified finished product — confirm the chain of custody and final product claim.
- Certificate numbers, validity dates and scanned copies are available on request, according to the website.

## What the certifications cover

- FSC Chain of Custody applies to paper and board products: rigid boxes, drawer boxes, hangtags and tissue paper, when produced with FSC-certified material.
- GRS applies to recycled content of polyester velvet and other synthetic fabrics; it verifies recycled content, not that the finished pouch is fully recycled or compostable.
- GOTS eligibility applies to organic cotton fibre; final product certification and transaction certificates must be checked separately.
- ISO 9001 is a quality-management system certification, not a product test or a guarantee of every unit.
- BSCI is a social-compliance audit programme, not a product-quality certificate.

## EU PPWR context

- The EU Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR) entered into force in February 2025; most obligations generally apply from 12 August 2026 according to the site's guide.
- Brands selling into the EU should prioritize mono-material, recyclable structures and avoid multi-material laminates that cannot be separated for recycling.
- Genesis offers recyclable mono-material structures, plastic-free options, biodegradable/compostable substrates and reusable/refillable designs, subject to the specific product.
- Material traceability documentation can be provided for FSC-certified paper/board.
- PPWR compliance depends on the final packaging design, material combination, recyclability, labelling and the brand's producer obligations; a supplier's material statement alone does not make a finished pack compliant.

## Eco material options

- FSC-certified paper and board.
- GRS-certified recycled polyester velvet (typically a modest premium over standard polyester velvet).
- GOTS-eligible organic cotton and GRS-certified recycled cotton variants.
- Natural-fibre cotton and linen pouches; paper-based accessories.
- Recyclable mono-material, reduced-plastic and plastic-free structures on request.
- Certificate/chain-of-custody documents should be requested early so they are ready with the shipment.

## Safe wording for customer emails

Until the current certificate is checked, use wording such as:

- "We can supply FSC-certified paper/board options and provide the chain-of-custody documentation for the specific order."
- "GRS-certified recycled fabric options are available on request; the exact scope is confirmed in the quotation."
- "Our team can recommend PPWR-oriented mono-material structures for your target market."

Do not claim that a finished package is "PPWR certified" or "fully compliant" without a documented assessment. Do not quote a certificate number or validity date unless it has been verified.
