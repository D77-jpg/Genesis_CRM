# Genesis Bags — Commercial Terms: MOQ, Samples, Lead Time, Payment, Shipping and Indicative Pricing

Source: https://www.genesisbags.com/ homepage FAQ, /blog/custom-packaging-pricing-guide/, /blog/packaging-production-timeline/, captured 2026-10-26.

IMPORTANT: This is the published website guidance, not a quotation or a binding offer. Confirm the exact price, material, quantity, production slot, Incoterm and payment schedule in the written quotation for each order. Prices and lead times can change with specification, season and factory capacity.

## MOQ

- Standard MOQ starts from 100 pcs.
- Standard designs across most categories — rigid gift boxes, fabric pouches, mailer boxes and paper bags — qualify for the 100-piece minimum.
- Fully customised fabric products (Pantone colour matching or embroidery) start from 500 pcs.
- Fully custom rigid box structures start from 300 pcs.
- Custom paper shopping bags typically start at 300–500 pcs per size/design because of rope-handle/gusseted tooling.
- Each size, colour and closure is a separate SKU with its own MOQ; multiple SKUs can be combined in one purchase order and consolidated shipment.

## Samples

- Standard-style physical samples of 5–10 pcs are free of charge; the buyer covers shipping.
- Custom samples are charged at cost and are refundable against the first bulk order of 500+ pieces.
- Physical samples are typically ready in 3–7 working days after artwork confirmation.
- Complex custom structures with new tooling can take 7–12 working days to sample.
- All customisation is confirmed via a physical sample before bulk production.

## Lead time

Published guidance:

- Quote and artwork: about 1–3 days after a clear specification.
- Sample: 3–7 working days after artwork confirmation; 7–12 working days for complex custom structures.
- Mass production, fabric pouches: 10–15 working days after sample approval and receipt of the 30% deposit.
- Mass production, rigid gift boxes: 15–20 working days after sample approval and deposit.
- Orders over 20,000 pcs: 20–30 working days.
- Air express (DHL/FedEx/UPS): 3–5 working days to most countries; the production timeline page also cites 3–7 days.
- Sea freight (FCL/LCL): 15–30 days on the homepage FAQ; the production timeline page cites 25–40 days.
- End-to-end standard air-express order: roughly 3–4 weeks; sea freight: roughly 6–8 weeks.
- Chinese New Year and October Golden Week can close factories for 1–2 weeks; plan buffer.

## Payment

- Standard payment terms: 30% T/T deposit before production and 70% T/T balance before shipment.
- Orders placed through the Alibaba store are eligible for Trade Assurance (Alibaba payment protection covering non-delivery or quality issues).
- PayPal is accepted for sample payments.
- Other accepted methods: bank transfer (T/T), Western Union and credit card via the Alibaba storefront.
- Exact payment schedule and currency must be stated in the quotation/contract.

## Shipping and Incoterms

- Air express: DHL, FedEx, UPS; fastest for samples and urgent/light orders.
- Air freight: middle option for medium loads.
- Sea freight: FCL/LCL; lowest unit cost for large/heavy orders, especially rigid boxes.
- Incoterms available: FOB Xiamen, EXW factory, CIF destination port.
- Genesis can arrange freight or ship on the customer's courier account.
- Shipments include a commercial invoice, packing list and certificate of origin.
- Duties/taxes at destination are separate from the product price.

## Indicative price ranges

These are website guidance for standard customisation at moderate volume, not a quotation:

- Fabric pouches (velvet, satin, cotton, linen, organza): from USD 0.25–1.50/pc.
- Rigid gift boxes: from USD 0.80–6.00/pc.
- Mailer boxes: from USD 0.50–1.80/pc.
- Paper shopping bags: from USD 0.30–1.20/pc.
- Jewelry example at 500–1,000 pcs: branded velvet pouch about USD 0.40–1.20; drawer box + insert + pouch about USD 1.80–4.00; magnetic box + foam insert + branded set about USD 3.00–7.00.

Price is driven by material, size, quantity, customisation level, logo/finishing method and internal components. Meaningful volume breaks are typically at 500, 1,000, 5,000 and 10,000 pcs. One-time screen/foil plate or die charges, freight, duties, certification premiums and inspection fees may be separate. Request an itemised quote.

## How to qualify a quote

To avoid three rounds of revisions, provide: product type and dimensions, target quantity, material preference, logo/print method (or ask for a recommendation), destination market and any certification requirement (for example FSC for the EU). The website states a 24-hour quote response target.
