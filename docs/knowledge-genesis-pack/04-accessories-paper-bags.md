# Genesis Bags — Packaging Accessories, Paper Bags and Complete Sets

Source: https://www.genesisbags.com/products/packaging-accessories/, /products/paper-shopping-bags/, /products/custom-solutions-oem-odm/, captured 2026-10-26.

## Packaging accessories

Genesis supplies accessories alongside pouches and boxes so a brand can order a coordinated packaging suite:

- Custom-printed hangtags in any shape.
- Branded satin or grosgrain ribbon, by the roll or pre-cut to length.
- Branded tissue paper.
- Foam dividers and fabric-lined inserts.
- Gift cards, envelopes and wax seals.
- Woven labels and other finishing items.

Accessories can be ordered with the main pouch or box order in one purchase order and one shipment. Standard MOQ is 100 pcs per item for standard designs; custom die-cut shapes may require a higher MOQ depending on complexity. Pantone colour matching is available across ribbon, tissue paper and hangtag stock.

## Paper shopping bags

Paper shopping bags are available as a custom special order rather than a stocked line:

- Rope-handle kraft bags, canvas totes and non-woven bags.
- Typically ordered with rigid or magnetic boxes for a complete retail presentation.
- FSC-certified kraft and uncoated paper stock can be available on request.
- Custom paper bags typically start at 300–500 pcs per size/design because of tooling for rope-handle or gusseted structures.
- Sampling is typically 3–7 days; bulk production is typically 15–20 working days, similar to rigid boxes.
- Paper bags can ship together with a box or pouch order in one consolidated shipment.

## Complete packaging sets

Genesis positions itself as a one-stop packaging factory. A typical complete set can combine:

- Primary layer: velvet, satin, cotton, linen, organza or microfiber pouch.
- Presentation layer: magnetic, rigid, drawer or book-shaped box.
- Protection layer: die-cut foam, velvet/EVA tray, cardboard divider or ribbon pull.
- Brand layer: hangtag, ribbon, tissue paper, label, envelope or wax seal.
- Carry/ship layer: paper shopping bag or mailer box where available.

One supplier and one purchase order can keep brand colour, logo treatment and quality consistent across the suite. Exact combination, MOQ and lead time depend on each item and must be confirmed in the quotation.

## OEM/ODM and private label

Genesis offers full OEM/ODM: structural design or refinement of the customer's die-line, material selection, Pantone matching, logo/finishing methods and production management from first sample to final shipment. The team can develop a structural concept from a rough brief or reference image, not only from finished artwork. Tooling for unique structures is a one-time cost and is typically amortised at 300–500 pcs; standard catalogue sizes need no new tooling and can start at 100 pcs.
