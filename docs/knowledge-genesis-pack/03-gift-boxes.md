# Genesis Bags — Rigid, Magnetic and Drawer Gift Boxes

Source: https://www.genesisbags.com/products/rigid-gift-box/, /products/magnetic-gift-box/, /products/sliding-drawer-box/, /products/custom-gift-boxes/, captured 2026-10-26.

Genesis manufactures rigid gift boxes, magnetic flip-lid boxes and sliding drawer boxes in its own Xiamen facility. The categories share board, printing, foil, embossing and insert capabilities, but differ in structure and opening experience.

## Magnetic gift box

- Structure: rigid box with a hidden magnetic flip-lid closure.
- Typical uses: cosmetic and skincare sets, jewelry, watches, corporate gifts and subscription boxes.
- Standard MOQ 100 pcs for standard structural sizes; fully custom structural designs from 300 pcs.
- Finishes: hot-foil stamping, embossing/debossing, spot UV gloss, matte and soft-touch lamination; combinable on one box.
- Inserts: foam, cardboard or velvet-lined inserts and dividers for multi-item sets.

## Rigid gift box

- Structures: two-piece lid-and-base, book-shaped flip box and window display box; fully bespoke structures available.
- Typical uses: premium retail products, jewelry, watches, gift sets, cosmetics and corporate gifts.
- Standard MOQ 100 pcs for standard structural sizes; fully custom structures from 300 pcs.
- Finishes: hot-foil stamping, embossing/debossing, spot UV gloss, matte and soft-touch lamination, all in-house.
- Inserts: foam, cardboard or velvet-lined inserts and dividers.
- Structural samples: typically 5–7 working days; mass production typically 15–20 working days after sample approval.

## Sliding drawer box

- Structure: sleeve-and-tray, matchbox-style drawer with a slide-open reveal.
- Typical uses: candles, tea, cosmetics, small gift sets and premium retail products.
- Standard MOQ 100 pcs for standard structural sizes; fully custom structures from 300 pcs.
- Finishes: hot-foil stamping, embossing/debossing, spot UV gloss, matte and soft-touch lamination.
- Every drawer box is built to the customer's product dimensions; no fixed-size template.
- Structural samples typically 5–7 working days; mass production typically 15–20 working days after sample approval.

## Inserts and internal presentation

Available insert types include foam, cardboard, velvet-lined trays and dividers. For jewelry, precision die-cut velvet or EVA trays can be sized to a ring, necklace or bracelet SKU. For watches, foam or velvet cushions can be die-cut to the case diameter and strap width. For multi-item sets, inserts and dividers hold each item in place.

## Box selection guidance

- Magnetic flip-lid: premium reveal for cosmetics, jewelry and gift sets.
- Two-piece lidded rigid: classic presentation for retail and jewelry.
- Book-shaped flip: a book-like opening for watches, jewelry and premium gifts.
- Sliding drawer: compact reveal for rings, candles, tea and small sets.
- Window display: product visible through a window while protected by a rigid structure.
