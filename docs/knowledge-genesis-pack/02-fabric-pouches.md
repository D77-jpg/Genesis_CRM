# Genesis Bags — Fabric Pouches and Drawstring Bags

Source: https://www.genesisbags.com/products/ and the individual pouch category pages (velvet, satin, cotton, linen, organza, microfiber), captured 2026-10-26.

Genesis manufactures six fabric pouch families. Each size, colour and closure is a separate SKU with its own MOQ. Multiple sizes and colours can be combined in one purchase order and one consolidated shipment.

## Velvet pouch

- Polyester velvet and cotton velvet with a deep pile; available in 20+ standard colours, with Pantone matching from 500 pcs.
- Typical uses: jewelry pouches, ring and earring pouches, wine and spirit bottle bags, coin purses, drawstring gift pouches and flat envelope pouches.
- Sizes: from 5×7 cm up to wine-bottle format; custom sizes available.
- Logo methods: embroidery (raised texture; most popular on velvet), hot-foil stamping (metallic finish) and screen printing (flat colour).
- Standard MOQ 100 pcs for existing sizes/colours; custom Pantone or unique structural dimensions from 500 pcs.
- In-house embroidery and hot-foil equipment; free embroidery digitising on orders over 300 pcs.

## Satin pouch

- High-sheen polyester satin in 20+ standard colours; Pantone matching from 500 pcs.
- Typical uses: wedding favour bags, beauty and cosmetic gift pouches, jewelry pouches and promotional drawstring pouches.
- Logo methods: screen printing for smooth satin and hot-foil stamping for a metallic finish.
- Standard MOQ 100 pcs; custom Pantone or unique structure from 500 pcs.

## Cotton pouch

- Natural cotton canvas and muslin; GOTS-eligible organic cotton and GRS-certified recycled cotton variants are available on request.
- Typical uses: drawstring pouches, muslin gift bags, canvas pouches and flat cotton bags.
- Logo method: in-house screen printing; the smooth woven surface takes single- and multi-colour logos.
- Standard MOQ 100 pcs; custom Pantone or unique dimensions from 500 pcs.
- Certified organic/recycled material sourcing can require a longer lead time.

## Linen pouch

- Natural linen and burlap in 12+ standard colours; rustic textured finish.
- Typical uses: wedding favour bags, farm-to-table food gifting and eco-positioned gift bags.
- Drawstring: jute or cotton cord.
- Logo methods: screen printing on woven linen; hot stamping possible on smoother linen blends after a material-specific recommendation.
- Standard MOQ 100 pcs; custom Pantone or unique dimensions from 500 pcs.

## Organza pouch

- Lightweight sheer organza mesh in 20+ standard colours.
- Typical uses: wedding favour bags, jewelry display pouches and small gift pouches.
- Closure: satin ribbon or matching cord drawstring.
- Because organza is sheer, Genesis typically uses a printed fabric label or heat-transfer design rather than printing directly on the mesh.
- Standard MOQ 100 pcs; custom Pantone from 500 pcs.

## Microfiber pouch

- Micro-denier polyester suede, brushed on both sides; scratch-safe for jewelry, watches and electronics.
- 15+ standard colours; Pantone matching from 500 pcs.
- Formats: single pouch, jewelry roll, fold-out travel organizer, cable/tech accessory pouch and card holder.
- Closures: snap, zip, tie cord or elastic band.
- Logo methods: debossing or heat-press foil (most popular), screen printing for full-colour designs and woven label patches.
- Standard MOQ 100 pcs per size/colour; multi-slot organizer styles can need extra sampling time.

## Pouch selection guidance

- Velvet is the default premium jewelry packaging material; its pile protects surfaces and signals value.
- Satin is lighter and glossier, suited to wedding, beauty and gift-with-purchase programmes.
- Organza is the sheer, lower-cost choice for high-volume favours and display.
- Cotton gives a smooth printable surface for eco and retail programmes; linen gives a natural, rustic texture.
- Microfiber suede is appropriate for scratch-safe travel organizers, watch/accessory rolls and tech pouches.

## Ordering facts to confirm

Standard MOQ is 100 pcs per colour/size for standard designs. Custom Pantone matching, unique dimensions or fully custom fabric programmes commonly start at 500 pcs. Samples are available before bulk production. Exact price, material grade, artwork setup and lead time must be confirmed in the written quotation.
