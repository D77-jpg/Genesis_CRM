# Genesis Bags — Buyer FAQ and Qualification Guide

Source: https://www.genesisbags.com/ homepage FAQ and product pages, captured 2026-10-26.

This is a sales-facing qualification reference. For binding answers use the current written quotation, certificate documents and approved commercial terms. Do not turn website ranges into a firm commitment without checking the order.

## Questions buyers ask

**What is your MOQ?**
Standard designs start from 100 pcs. Fully custom fabric (Pantone or embroidery) commonly starts from 500 pcs; fully custom rigid structures commonly start from 300 pcs. Each size/colour/closure is a separate SKU, but SKUs can be combined in one order and shipment.

**How fast can you sample?**
Standard samples: 3–7 working days after artwork confirmation. Complex custom structures: 7–12 working days. Standard-style samples of 5–10 pcs are free; buyer pays shipping. Custom samples are charged at cost and refundable against the first bulk order of 500+ pieces.

**How long is production?**
Fabric pouches: 10–15 working days after sample approval and the 30% deposit. Rigid gift boxes: 15–20 working days. Orders over 20,000 pcs: 20–30 working days. Air express adds about 3–7 days; sea freight adds about 15–40 days depending on route.

**Can you make a custom size and structure?**
Yes. Genesis builds to the customer's product dimensions, including bespoke rigid structures. A physical sample is required before bulk production. New structural tooling is a one-time cost and is typically amortised at 300–500 pcs.

**What logo methods can you do?**
Embroidery, hot-foil stamping, screen printing, heat transfer, debossing/embossing, spot UV and soft-touch/matte lamination, depending on the material. Velvet is best with embroidery or hot foil; satin with screen print or foil; cotton with screen print; organza with a printed label/heat transfer; microfiber with debossing/heat-press foil; rigid boxes with foil, emboss, spot UV and lamination.

**Can you match our Pantone colour?**
Yes, Pantone colour matching is offered. On fabric, it commonly starts at 500 pcs; on rigid boxes, custom structures/Pantone commonly start at 300 pcs. A physical sample or press proof is the approval reference, not a screen image.

**Can you supply boxes, pouches and bags together?**
Yes. Genesis positions itself as a one-stop packaging factory and can combine pouches, rigid/magnetic/drawer boxes, accessories, paper bags and inserts in one purchase order and one consolidated shipment, subject to each item's MOQ and tooling.

**What are your payment terms?**
Published standard: 30% T/T deposit before production, 70% T/T balance before shipment. Alibaba orders can be eligible for Trade Assurance. PayPal is accepted for sample payments. Exact terms must be in the quotation/contract.

**What shipping and Incoterms do you offer?**
Air express (DHL/FedEx/UPS), air freight and sea freight (FCL/LCL). Incoterms: FOB Xiamen, EXW factory, CIF destination port. Genesis can arrange freight or use the customer's courier account. Shipments include commercial invoice, packing list and certificate of origin.

**Do you offer FSC/GRS/GOTS/PPWR options?**
FSC-certified paper/board, GRS-certified recycled fabric and GOTS-eligible organic cotton are offered subject to material and certificate scope. PPWR-oriented mono-material, plastic-free and recyclable structures are available on request. Certificate numbers and validity dates must be verified from current documents; do not claim finished-pack compliance without assessment.

**What affects price?**
Material, size, quantity, customisation level, logo/finishing method and inserts. Website indicative ranges: fabric pouches USD 0.25–1.50/pc; rigid boxes USD 0.80–6.00/pc; mailer boxes USD 0.50–1.80/pc; paper bags USD 0.30–1.20/pc. Volume breaks commonly at 500/1,000/5,000/10,000 pcs. One-time plates/dies, freight, duties and inspection may be separate.

## Qualification checklist before quoting

1. Customer's product and dimensions (what goes inside).
2. Target quantity and whether multiple SKUs/colours are needed.
3. Material and finish preference; if unknown, recommend 1–2 options by use case.
4. Logo method and whether artwork is vector/ready.
5. Destination country and required certification (FSC/GRS/GOTS/PPWR).
6. Target launch date; work backwards through sample, production and freight.
7. Whether they need a complete suite (box + pouch + bag + accessories).

## Red flags / things not to promise

- Do not promise a price or lead time not confirmed in the quotation.
- Do not call a finished package "PPWR compliant" without a documented assessment.
- Do not claim a certificate number/validity without the current certificate.
- Do not imply that a website description or customer request is a supplier commitment.
- Do not claim a sample is free if it is a fully custom sample; custom samples are charged at cost.
- Do not treat a customer's target price or competitor price as Genesis's accepted price.
