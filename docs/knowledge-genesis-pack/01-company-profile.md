# Genesis Bags — Company Profile

Source: https://www.genesisbags.com/about/ (published website, captured 2026-10-26)

Genesis (Xiamen) Bags Co., Ltd. is a factory-direct custom bags and packaging manufacturer based in Xiamen, China. The company is built around its own drawstring pouch factory and in-house printing facility.

## What Genesis makes

Genesis supplies custom fabric pouches (velvet, satin, cotton, linen, organza and microfiber), rigid gift boxes, magnetic closure boxes, sliding drawer boxes and packaging accessories. It also produces paper shopping bags as custom special orders.

## Factory and scale

- Self-owned factory: 5,000 m² in Xiamen, Fujian.
- 120+ skilled staff.
- 5 million+ units annual capacity.
- Factory operating and exporting since 2014.
- Exports to 30+ countries across North America, Europe, Australia, the Middle East and Southeast Asia.
- One in-house factory covers offset printing, die-cutting, foil stamping, gluing, assembly and final QC.

## Certifications and audits

Genesis states that it holds ISO 9001 quality management, FSC® Chain of Custody and BSCI social-compliance certification/audit status. Certificate numbers, validity dates and scanned copies are available on request for verification. Website text alone is not a substitute for the current certificate document.

## Service model

- Full OEM/ODM support: customer artwork or Genesis structural design, Pantone colour matching and logo/finishing development.
- Factory-direct: no trading-company markup; one point of contact from sample to shipment.
- Standard MOQ from 100 pcs for standard designs. Fully custom rigid structures start from 300 pcs; fully custom fabric/Pantone or embroidery programmes start from 500 pcs.
- Website target response time: within 24 hours.
- Standard-style samples of 5–10 pcs are free of charge; buyer covers shipping. Custom samples are charged at cost and are refundable against the first bulk order of 500+ pieces.
