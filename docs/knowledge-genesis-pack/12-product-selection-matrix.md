# Genesis Bags — Product Selection Matrix by Customer Use Case

Source: Genesis product and industry pages, captured 2026-10-26. This is a recommendation aid, not a final technical specification.

| Customer product / use case | Primary recommendation | Optional presentation layer | Branding options | Key question to ask |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fine jewelry: rings, earrings, pendants | Velvet pouch; velvet/EVA fitted tray | Magnetic or rigid box | Hot foil, embroidery, deboss | Ring/necklace dimensions and whether pieces ship individually or as a set |
| Necklaces / coin pendants | Velvet or satin pouch | Book-shape or drawer box | Hot foil, screen print | Pendant size and whether the chain stays attached |
| Watches | Microfiber travel pouch or fitted cushion | Book-shape, drawer or magnetic box | Deboss, hot foil, soft-touch | Case diameter, strap width, single or multi-watch |

| Fashion accessories / dust bags | Cotton or canvas drawstring pouch | Branded hangtag/tissue | Screen print, woven label, embroidery | Bag/shoe dimensions and whether reusable |
| Corporate gifts | Magnetic/rigid box | Cotton pouch, ribbon, tissue | Foil, emboss, Pantone | Gift contents, target date, quantity, destination |
| Retail carry-out | Paper shopping bag | Coordinated box/tissue/ribbon | Foil, print, handle | Bag dimensions, load weight, paper/handle preference |

| Multi-SKU distributor order | Mixed pouch sizes/colours | Consolidated box shipment | Consistent logo/colour | SKU list, quantities per SKU, ship-to locations |

## Selection rules

- Start with what touches the product (primary protection), then add presentation.
- Do not put a heavy or rigid item in an undersized pouch; use a box and fitted insert.
- Use velvet for premium jewelry; satin for lighter/gift-forward pieces; organza for sheer, high-volume favors; cotton/linen for natural and reusable positioning; microfiber for scratch-safe travel.
- For a complete suite, keep one Pantone reference and one logo treatment across pouch, box, tissue and bag.
- If the customer's product dimensions are unknown, ask before proposing a structure; do not guess.

## Commercial checkpoints

- Standard MOQ 100 pcs; custom rigid structures 300 pcs; custom fabric Pantone/embroidery 500 pcs; paper bags 300–500 pcs.
- Sampling 3–7 working days standard, 7–12 complex.
- Fabric production 10–15 working days; rigid boxes 15–20 working days after sample and deposit.
- Price, freight, duties and certification premiums are quoted per order; website ranges are not a quote.
