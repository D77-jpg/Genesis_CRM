# Genesis Bags — Jewelry and Watch Packaging Solutions

## Jewelry packaging

Genesis designs and manufactures presentation packaging for jewelry brands: ring boxes, necklace cases, velvet jewelry pouches, fitted inserts, foil-stamped branding and premium unboxing. Production is factory-direct in Xiamen.

Common needs:

- Fitted inserts: precision die-cut velvet and EVA trays sized to the exact ring, necklace or bracelet SKU so the piece sits securely.
- Branding: gold, silver, rose or custom Pantone foil; soft-touch lamination, debossing and spot UV.
- Small batches: standard rigid boxes and velvet pouches can start from 100 pcs.
- Coordinated sets: jewelry clients often order a box, pouch and gift bag set so the unboxing is consistent.

Relevant product categories:

- Velvet pouch: soft-touch drawstring pouch for rings, earrings and pendants.
- Satin pouch: soft-sheen drawstring pouch for rings, bracelets and small sets.
- Microfiber pouch: suede-like travel organizers and rolls for jewelry; scratch-safe.
- Rigid gift box: two-piece, book-shape or magnetic box with fitted velvet/EVA inserts.
- Magnetic gift box: flip-lid reveal for premium jewelry sets.
- Sliding drawer box: compact drawer presentation for rings and small pieces.

Jewelry packaging facts:

- Standard rigid box structures can start from 100 pcs; fully custom structural designs or Pantone-matched colours commonly start from 300 pcs.
- Foam and velvet inserts can be die-cut to the customer's product dimensions, including multi-piece sets.
- Pantone colour matching and hot foil stamping in gold, silver, rose or custom colours are standard options on boxes and pouches.
- A coordinated box, pouch and gift bag set is common for jewelry clients.

## Watch packaging

Genesis produces rigid presentation boxes for watches, with fitted cushions and pillow inserts.

Common needs:

- Fitted watch cushions: foam or velvet cushions die-cut and positioned to hold the case and strap securely without scuffing.
- Structures: book-shape flip box, two-piece rigid box, sliding drawer box and magnetic closure box.
- Multi-watch cases: collector cases can be produced with individually fitted cushions for two, three or more watches.
- Finishes: soft-touch lamination, debossed logos and gold/silver foil detailing.
- Cushion sizing: die-cut to the specified case diameter and strap width.
- Standard rigid structures can start from 100 pcs; fully custom structural designs commonly start from 300 pcs.

Relevant categories: rigid gift box, sliding drawer box, packaging accessories (foam/fabric cushions, ribbon pulls and authentication cards), microfiber pouch for travel rolls and organizers.

## Use-case selection

- Entry jewelry piece: branded velvet or microfiber pouch.
- Higher-value jewelry set: magnetic or rigid box with fitted velvet/EVA insert and coordinated pouch.
- Watch: book-shape or drawer box with fitted cushion; multi-watch collector case for several pieces.
- Gift-with-purchase: organza or satin pouch in brand colour.
- Retail carry-out: paper shopping bag coordinated with the box.
