# Genesis Bags — Beauty, Fashion, Corporate Gifting and Retail Solutions

Source: https://www.genesisbags.com/cosmetic-packaging/, /fashion-accessories-packaging/, /corporate-gifting-packaging/, /luxury-retail-packaging/, captured 2026-10-26.

## Beauty and skincare

Genesis makes launch-ready packaging for beauty and skincare brands:

- Magnetic flip-lid boxes with foam or velvet inserts for skincare sets, sample kits and limited-edition collections.
- Satin pouches for gift sets, makeup bundles and gift-with-purchase.
- FSC-certified board, recyclable mono-material structures and plastic-free options for EU PPWR-oriented projects.
- Samples in 3–7 days and mass production in 10–15 days are the published launch timeline.
- Internal dividers, foam trays or fitted paperboard inserts can hold multiple SKUs securely.
- Finishes include holographic foil, soft-touch lamination, spot UV and embossing.
- Standard designs from 100 pcs; fully custom rigid box structures from 300 pcs; fabric Pantone matching from 500 pcs.

Typical starting point: magnetic gift box plus fitted insert; add satin pouch for gift-with-purchase or travel/sample kits.

## Fashion and accessories

Genesis produces branded dust bags and drawstring pouches for fashion and accessories:

- Materials: cotton, canvas, satin, velvet, organza and non-woven; chosen to match the product's price point and brand feel.
- Common products: dust bags for handbags, shoes and small leather goods; drawstring pouches for scarves, sunglasses and jewelry; garment pouches.
- Branding: screen printing, heat transfer, embroidery and woven labels.
- Standard MOQ 100 pcs for standard drawstring pouches and dust bags in stock fabrics/colours; higher MOQ for fully custom Pantone fabric.
- Cotton, canvas and other reusable/recyclable fabrics are available for brands reducing single-use packaging.
- Size is made to the customer's specific product; a physical sample confirms fit.

Typical starting point: cotton or canvas dust bag; add woven label, hangtag and tissue paper for a coordinated set.

## Corporate gifting

Genesis handles bulk, branded corporate gift orders:

- Magnetic and rigid gift boxes for client gifts, employee holiday sets, onboarding kits and event giveaways.
- Consistent quality and branding across large orders; mixed-SKU gift sets can be held with fitted foam or card inserts.
- Hot-foil logo stamping, embossing and Pantone colour matching keep the brand presentation consistent at volume.
- Standard MOQ 100 pcs for standard rigid/magnetic structures; 300 pcs for fully custom structures or Pantone-matched colours.
- For event gifting, the published recommendation is to start the sample process at least 6–8 weeks before the target date, because bulk production runs 15–20 working days after sample approval and deposit.

Typical starting point: magnetic gift box with fitted insert, plus cotton pouch or accessories for an eco-positioned set.

## Luxury retail and boutique

Genesis supplies the retail packaging suite:

- Shopping bags, rigid gift boxes, tissue paper, ribbon, hangtags and inserts can be made in-house and coordinated.
- Matched colours, logo placement and finishes across bags, boxes and accessories.
- Structures for in-store handling and direct-to-consumer shipping.
- FSC-certified paper and recyclable mono-material structures are available across bags, boxes and mailer packaging.
- Standard designs from 100 pcs; fully custom sizes or structures from 500 pcs.
- Split shipments and freight coordination to multiple destinations or a fulfilment warehouse are available.
- A full suite (bag, box, tissue, ribbon) can be ordered in one purchase order for consistent branding.

Typical starting point: coordinated run of rigid/magnetic boxes, paper shopping bags, tissue paper and ribbon.
