# Genesis Bags — Customization, Logo Methods and Sampling

Source: https://www.genesisbags.com/ (homepage FAQ and customization section), /blog/logo-methods-custom-pouches/, /products/custom-solutions-oem-odm/, captured 2026-10-26.

## Dimensions, colours and materials

- Size and shape: any dimension, including bespoke structural design based on the customer's product dimensions.
- Colour: 20+ standard options; Pantone colour matching is available. Pantone matching on fabric commonly starts from 500 pcs; custom rigid structures commonly start from 300 pcs.
- Materials: premium rigid board, FSC-certified kraft/paper, velvet, satin, cotton, linen, organza, microfiber and non-woven fabrics.
- Internal components: velvet lining, foam inserts, ribbon pulls, magnetic closures, dividers and die-cut trays.

## Logo methods

Genesis offers these branding methods, with the best choice depending on material and artwork:

1. **Embroidery** — raised, tactile, permanent logo on velvet, suede and canvas. Best for simple text/monograms; complex gradients are not possible. Typical logo volume from 500 pcs. One-time digitisation fee; free digitising on orders over 300 pcs for velvet.
2. **Hot-foil stamping / hot stamping** — metallic or pigment foil pressed onto velvet, satin or suede with a heated die. Single colour per pass; gold, silver, rose gold and custom foils. Standard MOQ from 100 pcs; one-time die cost.
3. **Screen printing** — ink through a mesh screen; best on cotton, satin, canvas and other smooth fabrics. Economical for 1–3 colours; each colour needs a screen. Not recommended for velvet's deep pile.
4. **Heat transfer** — full-colour, photographic-quality transfer for cotton, satin and nylon; no screen/die required, suitable for short runs and complex designs. Slightly plastic feel and can crack with repeated laundering; suitable for non-washed packaging pouches.
5. **Debossing / embossing** — recessed or raised logo on paper board, rigid boxes, suede and some fabrics.
6. **Spot UV** — gloss highlight on a printed box surface.
7. **Soft-touch / matte lamination** — surface finish for rigid and magnetic boxes.

## Artwork requirements

- Embroidery: vector file (AI/EPS) or high-resolution PNG (minimum 300 DPI at final print size) for digitisation.
- Hot stamping: vector AI or EPS to make the metal die; very fine hairlines below 0.5 mm may not reproduce reliably.
- Screen printing: vector or high-resolution raster at print size.
- Heat transfer: print-ready artwork at 300 DPI at final size.
- Artwork and Pantone references should be finalized before sampling to avoid delays.

## Sampling and approval

- Standard-style physical samples: typically 3–7 working days after artwork confirmation; complex custom structures with new tooling can take 7–12 days.
- Standard-style samples of 5–10 pieces are free; buyer covers shipping. Custom samples are charged at cost and refundable against the first bulk order of 500+ pieces.
- Customisation is confirmed through a physical sample before bulk production. The sample is the shared reference for material, colour, structure, logo position and finish.

## Practical fit guidance

- Velvet: use hot foil or embroidery; avoid full-colour direct printing because the pile gives uneven ink coverage.
- Satin: screen printing and hot foil both work; screen printing suits multi-colour flat artwork.
- Cotton: screen printing is the default for crisp, durable logos.
- Linen: screen printing is primary; hot stamping only on smoother blends after a material test.
- Organza: printing directly on mesh is unreliable; use a printed label or heat transfer.
- Microfiber suede: debossing or heat-press foil is the cleanest logo; woven labels and screen printing are alternatives.
- Rigid/magnetic boxes: hot foil, embossing/debossing, spot UV, matte/soft-touch lamination; multiple finishes can be combined.

## Important qualification

Logo feasibility, minimum size, colour accuracy and durability depend on the actual material, artwork and production method. A digital mock-up is not a colour match; a physical sample or press proof is required for approval.
